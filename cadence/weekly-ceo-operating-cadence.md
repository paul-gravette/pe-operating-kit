# Weekly CEO Operating Cadence

A default week for CEOs (PE-backed or high-growth). Adjust times; keep the **sequence**.

**Owner:** CEO  
**Week of:**  

## Monday — Orient (90–120 min)

- [ ] Review last week’s outcomes vs the three priorities
- [ ] Cash / pipeline / customer risk scan (15 min max)
- [ ] Set this week’s top 3 outcomes (one sentence each)
- [ ] Unblock people: list decisions only you can make
- [ ] Send a short leadership note (async beats a status meeting)

## Tuesday — Deep work on value drivers

- [ ] One block (2–3 hrs) on the #1 initiative — no meetings inside it
- [ ] Customer or product truth: talk to reality, not slides
- [ ] Hiring / talent move if open roles are on the critical path

## Wednesday — People & org

- [ ] 1:1s with direct reports (decisions + coaching, not therapy)
- [ ] One hard conversation you’ve been delaying (if needed)
- [ ] Org health: who’s overloaded, who’s underused

## Thursday — External & board readiness

- [ ] Investor / board / PE touchpoint if on cadence
- [ ] Partner, customer, or key hire conversations
- [ ] Draft or update the board one-pager if a meeting is ≤10 days out

## Friday — Close the loop

- [ ] Score the week: hit / miss / learn on the three outcomes
- [ ] Kill or defer one thing that isn’t earning its place
- [ ] Preview next week’s priorities with the LT (async OK)
- [ ] Personal shutdown: what only you carry into the weekend (ideally: nothing operational)

## Standing rules

1. **No meeting without an owner and a decision** (or cancel it).
2. **Metrics reviewed on a cadence or deleted** from the pack.
3. **Protect one deep-work block** even in chaotic weeks.
4. **Bad news early** — to the board and to yourself.

## Optional: 15-minute Sunday scan

Glance at calendar + cash + open asks. Don’t work the business; just remove Monday fog.
