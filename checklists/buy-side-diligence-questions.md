# Buy-Side Diligence Questions

Use in first management meetings and data-room reviews. Goal: force **pursue / pause / pass** — not generate a novel.

**Date:**

**Stage:** (IOI / LOI / confirmatory)

## Business quality

1. Who is the economic buyer, and what job are they hiring the product/service to do?
2. Why do customers stay? Why do they leave? (Get names, not averages.)
3. What would a well-funded competitor do in 12 months that would hurt you most?
4. Where is pricing power real vs. hoped-for? Last three price actions and outcomes?
5. What must be true for growth to continue at the rate in the CIM?

## Unit economics & financial truth

6. Contribution margin by segment / channel — where is profit actually made?
7. Working capital: what breaks if growth accelerates 30%?
8. Quality of earnings: one-time items, related parties, aggressive revenue cutoffs?
9. Capex / maintenance reality vs. “growth investment” labeling?
10. Cash conversion cycle and any supplier or customer financing dependency?

## Customers & concentration

11. Top 10 customers: % of revenue, tenure, NPS/proxy, renewal terms?
12. Any customer that could force a bad renegotiation in the next 18 months?
13. Pipeline: weighted vs. unweighted, and historical conversion honesty?

## People & org

14. Who leaves if the deal closes? Who becomes more valuable?
15. Key-person risk: name the people the model can’t lose.
16. Incentive alignment today vs. what we need post-close?
17. Culture tells: how are bad news and missed numbers handled?

## Operations & systems

18. What is still held together by heroes and spreadsheets?
19. Capacity constraints (people, plants, licenses, channel) at 1.5× volume?
20. Cyber / compliance / regulatory landmines that kill deals late?

## Thesis & value creation

21. What are the **three** levers we would pull in the first 100 days?
22. What has management already tried that failed — and why?
23. Where will we disagree with management on priorities?
24. Exit: who buys this and why in 3–7 years? What must improve for that buyer?

## Deal structure & process

25. What is management optimizing for in this process (price, certainty, role)?
26. What would make *us* walk — write it down before emotional attachment?
